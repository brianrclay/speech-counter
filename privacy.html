Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 15, 2026

This policy describes how Speech Count ("the app") handles information. It applies to the app on the web and on iOS/Android.

Information you enter

Everything you type or tap - student names, target names, tallies, totals, and the saved session history for each student - is stored in your browser's or device's local storage, on your own device. The free board never sends any of it anywhere, and using it requires no name, email, or account. If you use the Export feature, the file it produces is created on your device and goes only where you choose to save or share it.

Roster: purchases, sign-in, and sync

Roster is an optional purchase. Buying it, and optionally signing in, involves three services:

  • Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store), Google (Google Play), or on the web by Stripe through RevenueCat. We never see your card details. RevenueCat keeps a record of your purchase against a random identifier (your Support ID), and your email address if you sign in, so your purchase can be restored on your other devices. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Signing in is optional and uses only your email address: we email you a one-time code (sent through Resend, an email delivery service) and never store a password. Your email is stored with your purchase record and inside the sign-in token on your device.
  • Sync runs only while you're signed in. Your students and their sessions - the same information described above - are stored in your account on our hosting provider, Netlify, encrypted at rest, so they can appear on your other devices. We don't read, share, or sell this data, and we don't use it for anything but showing it back to you.

You can sign out at any time, which stops sync and keeps or removes the local copy as you choose. To delete the copy in your account, email us from the address you signed in with and we'll remove it.

Analytics

The app uses Google Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the app is used - for example how many people open it and which features are used - so we can improve it. Google Analytics automatically collects usage and interaction data, along with information such as your approximate location (derived from your IP address), device and browser type, and a pseudonymous identifier. This data is processed by Google on our behalf and is not linked to your name or the target names, tallies, or notes you enter in the app.

We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not sell it. For details on how Google handles information collected through Analytics, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can opt out of Google Analytics in your browser using Google's opt-out add-on. Apart from the services described under Roster above, no other third-party service is used by the app.

Data retention and your control

Your data stays on your device until you remove it yourself - by clearing rows in the app, clearing your browser's site data, or uninstalling the app. If you've used sync, the copy in your account stays until you ask us to delete it; purchase records are kept by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat under their own policies for as long as required for billing and refunds.

Children's privacy

Speech Count is intended for use by speech-language professionals, caregivers, and educators tracking someone else's practice sessions. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The analytics described above measure app usage in aggregate and are not used to identify any individual.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. When we do, this page will describe what information is collected and how it's used, and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.
