Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 13, 2026

This policy describes how Speech Count ("the app") handles information. It applies to the app on the web and on iOS/Android.

Information you enter

None of the information you enter in Speech Count is collected by us or sent anywhere. Everything you type or tap - target names, tallies, and totals - is stored only in your browser's or device's local storage, on your own device. We do not operate a server that receives, stores, or has access to this data, and we never ask for your name, email, or any other personal information.

Analytics

The app uses Google Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the app is used - for example how many people open it and which features are used - so we can improve it. Google Analytics automatically collects usage and interaction data, along with information such as your approximate location (derived from your IP address), device and browser type, and a pseudonymous identifier. This data is processed by Google on our behalf and is not linked to your name or the target names, tallies, or notes you enter in the app.

We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not sell it. For details on how Google handles information collected through Analytics, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can opt out of Google Analytics in your browser using Google's opt-out add-on. No other third-party service is used by the app today.

Data retention and your control

Your data stays on your device until you remove it yourself - by clearing rows in the app, clearing your browser's site data, or uninstalling the app. Since we never receive a copy, there is nothing on our end to delete.

Children's privacy

Speech Count is intended for use by speech-language professionals, caregivers, and educators tracking someone else's practice sessions. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The analytics described above measure app usage in aggregate and are not used to identify any individual.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes - for example, if we introduce optional accounts or paid features in the future. If that happens, this page will be updated to describe what information is collected and how it's used, and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.
