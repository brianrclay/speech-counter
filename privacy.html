Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 22, 2026

This policy describes how Speech Count ("the app") handles information. It applies to the app on the web and on iOS/Android.

Information you enter

Everything you type or tap - student names, target names, tallies, totals, and the saved session history for each student - is stored in your browser's or device's local storage, on your own device. The free board never sends any of it anywhere, and using it requires no name, email, or account. If you use the Export feature, the file it produces is created on your device and goes only where you choose to save or share it.

Speech Count Pro: purchases, sign-in, and sync

Speech Count Pro is an optional purchase. Buying or restoring Pro requires signing in with a verified email address and involves these services:

  • Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store), Google (Google Play), or on the web by Stripe through RevenueCat. We never see your card details. RevenueCat keeps a record of your purchase against a random identifier (your Support ID), and your email address if you sign in, so your purchase can be restored on your other devices. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Signing in is required for Pro purchases and sync, and uses only your email address: we email you a one-time code (sent through Resend, an email delivery service) and never store a password. Your email is stored with your purchase record and inside the sign-in token on your device.
  • Sync runs only while you're signed in. Your students and their sessions - the same information described above - are stored in your account on our hosting provider, Netlify, encrypted at rest, so they can appear on your other devices. We don't read, share, or sell this data, and we don't use it for anything but showing it back to you.

You can log out at any time, which stops sync and removes the roster from that device; it stays in your account and comes back when you log in. Deleting your account from the Account page removes the copy in your account immediately and signs out every device; any device that was offline at the time discards its copy the next time it tries to sync. You can also email us from the address you signed in with and we'll do the same.

When you delete a student or a session, the app keeps only that record's ID and the deletion time for 90 days - the name, target, and counts are erased right away - so that the deletion reaches your other devices. After 90 days the record is dropped entirely.

On a phone or tablet, the operating system's device backup (iCloud or Google backup) may include the app's data along with everything else on the device; that backup is governed by Apple's or Google's policies and your device settings.

Analytics

The app uses Google Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the app is used - for example how many people open it and which features are used - so we can improve it. Google Analytics automatically collects usage and interaction data, along with information such as your approximate location (derived from your IP address), device and browser type, and a pseudonymous identifier. This data is processed by Google on our behalf and is not sent with your name, email, or the student names, targets, tallies, or notes you enter in the app. Google's advertising features are turned off.

We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not sell it. For details on how Google handles information collected through Analytics, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can turn analytics off at any time with the "Share usage and purchase analytics" switch on the Account page, on the web and in the apps. Apart from the services described under Speech Count Pro above, no other third-party service is used by the app.

When web analytics is enabled and you sign in, we store the browser's pseudonymous Analytics identifier and your analytics preference with your Speech Count account. This lets us report verified web purchases, renewals, and refunds from RevenueCat to Google Analytics, including product, amount, currency, and transaction identifier. Your email, account ID, and student information are not sent to Google. Turning analytics off while signed in also stops future server-side revenue reporting for that account once the preference is saved. Account deletion removes this identifier. Native App Store and Google Play payments are not included in this web revenue integration.

Data retention and your control

Your data stays on your device until you remove it yourself - by deleting students and sessions in the app, logging out, clearing your browser's site data, or uninstalling the app. If you've used sync, the copy in your account stays until you delete your account or ask us to. Account deletion removes your email and its account lookup from Speech Count's account database, along with your synced roster and pending sign-in code. A record containing only the old random account ID and revocation time remains to reject old sign-in tokens. Deletion does not cancel recurring subscriptions; use Manage subscription to stop future charges. Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google, RevenueCat, and Stripe under their own policies for as long as required for billing, refunds, and tax.

Children's privacy

Speech Count is intended for use by speech-language professionals, caregivers, and educators tracking someone else's practice sessions. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children themselves. The students whose names and results an adult user enters may be children; that information is entered and controlled by the adult user, stored as described above, and used only to show it back to that user. The analytics described above measure app usage in aggregate and never include student information.

If you use Speech Count for students or clients covered by school or health privacy rules (such as FERPA or HIPAA), it's your responsibility to confirm the app fits your organisation's requirements before entering identifiable records; using initials or nicknames is one way to keep records de-identified. We don't currently sign business associate or school data agreements.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. When we do, this page will describe what information is collected and how it's used, and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.
