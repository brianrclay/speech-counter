Support

Speech Count for iOS and Android

Need help with Speech Count, found a bug, or have a feature request? Email brianrclay@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Adding and removing rows

Tap Add to create a new row for a target or activity. Each row has its own Name and Target fields, plus separate Correct and Incorrect counters. Tap Remove on a row to delete just that row.

Counting correct and incorrect

Tap the Correct or Incorrect button on a row to log a trial. The row's total and percent-correct update automatically as you go.

Undo and clear

Tapped the wrong button? Tap Undo to reverse your last action. Tap Clear to reset every row's counts back to zero and start a new session.

Where is my data?

Everything you enter is saved automatically on your own device and is still there the next time you open the app. Nothing is uploaded to a server. See our Privacy Policy for details.
