Student paywall concepts

Three takes on the pre-purchase Students screen, each built on the app's own stylesheet and illustrated with screenshots of the real app. Open one on a phone-width window to judge it fairly.

  • Concept A Swipe deck A horizontal, snap-scrolling deck of five numbered cards above the plans. Shortest page of the three; the visuals are opt-in rather than forced on the reader.
  • Concept B Feature strips One screenshot per claim, stacked full width, with the plans up top and a purchase bar that sticks as you scroll. The most complete argument and the longest page.
  • Concept C Before and after A single hero that shows a board row turning into a student record, then the plans, then the remaining features as small thumbnails.
